Admissions

  1. Send an enquiry. Fill in the form below and we will reply on WhatsApp.
  2. Visit the school. Meet the teachers and see the classrooms.
  3. Complete registration. Bring a birth certificate, immunisation card and previous report (if any). Grades 4 to 6 also need an assessment report and assessment number. Grades 7 to 9 also need the KEPSEA certificate.
  4. Welcome day. Your child settles in with a buddy.
